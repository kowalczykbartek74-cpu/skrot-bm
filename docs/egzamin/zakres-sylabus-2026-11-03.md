# Zakres a sylabus: lista do decyzji (skrotbm.pl, 3.11.2026)

Wygenerowane narzędziem „Zakres a sylabus” w Panelu AI na prawdziwych materiałach. Nic nie zostało usunięte.
Dopasowanie idzie po słowach tytułu, więc „bez sylabusa” często znaczy tylko, że temat z sylabusa jest opisany ogólniej
(np. jeden punkt sylabusa obejmuje kilka lekcji). Najbardziej wiarygodne są pary „dubel?” z tym samym tematem z sylabusa.

## Najpewniejsze duble (warto sprawdzić najpierw)

- **OWI:** „Basic principles of intellectual property law” ↔ „Introduction to Intellectual Property Protection” (ten sam temat: Basic principles of intellectual property law).
- **Prawo:** „Legal aspects of European integration” ↔ „The European Union: Integration, Institutions and the Court of Justice”.
- **Prawo:** „Introduction to Law: Legal Norms, Legal Systems and Branches of Law” nakłada się na „Legal norms, legal provisions and legal orders” i „Public and private law” (nie zgłoszone przez narzędzie, widać po tytułach).
- **Prawo:** „Sources of Law in Poland…” i „Sources of EU Law…”: w sylabusie nie ma osobnego tematu o źródłach prawa; to dodatkowe materiały, nie duble.
- **Podstawy zarządzania:** „Introduction to Management: Functions, Roles and Organizational Resources” ↔ „Introduction to Management: Concepts, Functions, Managers and Organizational Goals” ↔ „Management and the manager's tasks: functions, roles and skills”.
- **Podstawy zarządzania:** „Leadership in the organization” ↔ „Leadership, Managerial Competence and Human Resource Development”.
- **Makroekonomia:** „Measuring output: GDP, value added, real and nominal GDP” ↔ „Measuring the Economy: GDP and Its Calculation” ↔ „Measuring GDP: What Counts, Real vs Nominal, Deflator (problem set)”.
- **Makroekonomia:** „Economics, scarcity and the circular flow of income” ↔ „Introduction to Economics and Macroeconomic Foundations”.

Fałszywe alarmy narzędzia (do pominięcia): w Prawie „Law and technology” zestawione z materiałami o źródłach prawa (wspólne słowo „law”), w Logistyce „The essence of logistics” ↔ „Logistics costs”, w Analizie „Functions and elementary functions” ↔ „Asymptotes of a function”.

## Pełny wynik narzędzia

### Analiza matematyczna (wykład i ćwiczenia: zakres 20, sylabus 4)
Bez odpowiednika w sylabusie (sylabus ma tylko 4 ogólne tematy): Sequences, their limits and the number e; Limit of a function and continuity; Concavity, convexity and inflection points; L'Hospital's rule and indeterminate forms; Full investigation of a function and sketching its graph; Constrained extrema: substitution and Lagrange multipliers; Integration by substitution and by parts; Integrating rational functions; Improper integrals and perpetual income streams; Derivatives in economics: marginal values and elasticity; Integrals in economics: totals, surplus and present value.
Dubel?: Functions and elementary functions ↔ Asymptotes of a function; Monotonicity and extrema of a function ↔ Local extrema of functions of two variables; Functions of two variables and partial derivatives ↔ Local extrema of functions of two variables; Local extrema of functions of two variables ↔ Differential and Integral Calculus of One and Two Variables.

### Ekonomia biznesu (zakres 10, sylabus 7)
Bez odpowiednika: Demand, supply and market equilibrium; Elasticity of demand and pricing decisions; Production decisions (ćwiczenia); Short-run costs; Profit maximisation: MR = MC, break-even and shutdown; Perfect competition and monopoly; Monopolistic competition, oligopoly and strategic decisions.

### Logistyka (zakres 11, sylabus 5)
Bez odpowiednika: Inventory tools: ABC/XYZ analysis and inventory ratios; Logistics: course map from the syllabus; Warehouse management (ćwiczenia); Transport, load units and packaging (ćwiczenia).
Dubel?: The essence of logistics ↔ Logistics costs.

### Makroekonomia (wykład: zakres 14, sylabus 4; ćwiczenia: sylabus 5)
Bez odpowiednika: Economics, scarcity and the circular flow of income; Measuring output: GDP, value added, real and nominal GDP; Inflation: measurement, causes and costs; Public demand, fiscal policy and international trade; Measuring GDP: What Counts, Real vs Nominal, Deflator (problem set); Measuring the Economy: GDP and Its Calculation; Economic growth and the business cycle (ćw.); The labour market and unemployment (ćw.); The multiplier and equilibrium in the goods market (ćw.).
Dubel?: Economic growth and the business cycle ↔ Introduction to Economics and Macroeconomic Foundations; Economics, scarcity and the circular flow of income ↔ Introduction to Economics and Macroeconomic Foundations.

### Narzędzia IT w zarządzaniu (zakres 11, sylabus 6–7)
Bez odpowiednika: Normalization: 1NF, 2NF and 3NF; SQL basics; SQL: joins, aggregation and grouping; Market basket analysis (wykład); Information systems and IT tools in management (lab); Requirements analysis of an information system (lab); Cloud-based IT tools in management (lab).

### Ochrona własności intelektualnej (zakres 7, sylabus 5)
Bez odpowiednika: Trade marks, geographical indications and unfair competition.
Dubel?: Basic principles of intellectual property law ↔ Introduction to Intellectual Property Protection.

### Podstawy finansów (zakres 13, sylabus 6)
Bez odpowiednika: Debt management: loans, interest, APR and repayment; The young entrepreneur and taxes; Financial protection for the future: insurance, pensions and investing; The financial system (ćw.); The banking system (ćw.).

### Podstawy zarządzania (wykład: zakres 20, sylabus 7; ćwiczenia: sylabus 10)
Bez odpowiednika: The organization: essence, components and internal environment; The organization's environment and stakeholders; Planning and defining goals; Leadership in the organization; Managerial Problems and Organizational Design in Clothing Manufacturing; Leadership, Managerial Competence and Human Resource Development; Managerial Control, Efficiency, Financial Analysis and Economic Calculation; (ćw.) Management and the manager's tasks; The essence of strategy; Motivating and influencing employees; The controlling process; Process management: Lean, Six Sigma; Fundamentals of Management: Functions, Strategy, Structure, Control and Lean; Management in Transport, Forwarding and Logistics Companies; Introduction to Management (2 wersje).
Dubel?: The organization: essence… ↔ The organization's environment…; Management and the manager's tasks ↔ Introduction to Management: Concepts…; Fundamentals of Management… ↔ Introduction to Management: Functions, Roles…; Traditional and contemporary management ↔ Leadership in the organization; Leadership in the organization ↔ Leadership, Managerial Competence…; Introduction to Management: Functions, Roles… ↔ Introduction to Management: Concepts…

### Prawo (zakres 16, sylabus 11)
Dubel?: Law and technology ↔ Sources of EU Law / Application and Interpretation of Law / Sources of Law in Poland (fałszywy alarm); Legal aspects of European integration ↔ The European Union: Integration, Institutions and the Court of Justice; Sources of EU Law ↔ Sources of Law in Poland.

### Zarządzanie zasobami ludzkimi (wykład: zakres 12, sylabus 8; ćwiczenia: sylabus 15)
Bez odpowiednika: Job analysis and its use in recruitment; Recruitment: attracting the right candidates; Shaping careers in the organization; Organizing the HR function, new ways of HRM and current challenges; Selection and adaptation (onboarding) (ćw.).
